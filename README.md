## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Prompt Frameworks (Days 01–09)

### 🎯 Day 01: Foundational AI Positioning
*Objective: Build an explicit system persona and constraint layer to eliminate generic, generic text outputs.*

```text
"Act as an expert [Insert Role, e.g., Senior UX Copywriter]. I need to create a [Insert Output, e.g., landing page header] for [Insert Target Audience]. Focus strictly on highlighting [Insert Value Proposition]. Do not include buzzwords or filler language."
⚡ Day 02: The 3 C's Framework (Concise, Clear, Consistent)
Objective: Prevent context windows from degrading by keeping prompts highly dense, explicit, and contextual.

Example System Prompt with Reference:

Plaintext
"Write a 3-line email promoting our new software service to busy founders. Use the exact tone, paragraph breaks, and signature style shown in this reference text: [Insert Example Text]."
⛓️ Day 03: Advanced System Prompting (Power Phrases & Task-Chaining)
Objective: Break down complex operations into single, logical links where the output of prompt A feeds the input of prompt B.

Prompt 1 (The Ideation Link):

Plaintext
"Brainstorm 5 unique, low-cost marketing ideas for a local [Insert Business Type]. Think step-by-step and focus entirely on high-conversion social strategies."
Prompt 2 (The Chained Execution Link):

Plaintext
"Take idea #3 from your previous response. Play devil's advocate: identify its biggest operational bottleneck and build a 4-week execution timeline to safely mitigate that risk."
🛡️ Day 04: Responsible AI Workflows (The A-C-T Framework)
Objective: Ensure clean data privacy, structural compliance, and precise output auditing.

System Audit Prompt Template:

Plaintext
"Review the following generated text. Check explicitly for structural biases, factual inconsistencies, or logical hallucinations based purely on industry standards. Flag any statement that cannot be definitively backed up with standard methodology."
🤖 Day 05: Agentic Logic Systems (Multi-Step Task Delegation)
Objective: Move from direct prompt text generation into setting overarching goals for iterative execution.

Plaintext
"Your goal is to build a structured template for [Insert Mission, e.g., a customer onboarding process]. Operate in a continuous loop: Analyze our current parameters, draft the initial framework, observe potential execution friction points, and refine. Halt and request explicit human intervention if you hit an irreversible operational risk."
📊 Day 06: Iterative Product Ideation
Objective: Transition from standard brain-dumps into analytical matrices inside your chat environment.

Step 1: Ideation Partner

Plaintext
"You are a marketing strategist in the skincare space. My company is launching an advanced face serum. Help me brainstorm 5 high-margin bundle or add-on ideas I could offer with this launch."
Step 2: Analysis Matrix Generation

Plaintext
"Generate a structured markdown cost-benefit table for these 5 ideas. Include columns for: Estimated Implementation Complexity, Potential Profit Margin, and Scalability Score."
🔍 Day 07: Automated Risk Assessment
Objective: Extract structural flaws and hidden dependencies from your project blueprints before implementation.

Step 1: The Gap Finder

Plaintext
"You are an expert project manager. Review the following project brief for our upcoming launch: [Insert Brief Text]. Identify any hidden dependencies, unbacked timelines, or process gaps completely missing from this strategy."
Step 2: Risk Scoring Matrix

Plaintext
"Focus heavily on the [Insert Specific Gap Found]. Break down how this single variable could cascade into a major timeline blocker. Assign a specific risk score (Low, Medium, High) and provide a mitigation plan."
📈 Day 08: Multi-Prompt Research Pipelines
Objective: Build an end-to-end extraction pipeline to move from a raw trend into clean, cited, visually structured reports.

Step 1: High-Velocity Synthesis

Plaintext
"I am analyzing market positioning for [Insert Topic/Product]. What are the top 3 emerging industry trends shaking up this space over the last 12-24 months?"
Step 2: Deep Extraction & Citation (Best executed in Perplexity or Gemini Deep Research)

Plaintext
"Run a deep research dive into [Insert Trend From Step 1]. Pull concrete data points, industry percentages, and adoption rates. Provide clear, hyperlinked source citations for every data metric."
🧠 Day 09: Intelligent Document Contexts (NotebookLM Source Tutoring)
Objective: Run isolated data mining queries against your custom documents with zero internet hallucinations.

Prompt 1: The Single-Page Executive Distillation

Plaintext
"Analyze all uploaded documents within this workspace. Generate a strict one-page summary structured into three parts: 1. Core Objective. 2. Top 5 data-backed discoveries. 3. Immediate real-world applications. Append precise document source citations to every bullet point."
Prompt 2: The Structural Logic Auditor

Plaintext
"Act as a critical, high-level auditor. Scan these uploaded documents and map out any weak evidence, logic loops, unbacked industry assumptions, or conflicting assertions. Give me 3 hard questions that test the integrity of this text."
