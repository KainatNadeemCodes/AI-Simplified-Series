## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Prompt Frameworks

### 💡 Day 06: Product Brainstorming & Analysis Matrix
*Objective: Transition from messy brainstorming sessions to data-driven, structured decision tables.*

* **Step 1: Ideation Partner**
  ```text
  "You are a [marketing expert] in the [skincare industry]. My company is launching a [new face serum]. Help me brainstorm [bundle ideas or add-ons] I could offer with this product."
Step 2: Analysis

Plaintext
"Make a structured cost-benefit table for these ideas so I can identify the most scalable choice immediately."
🔍 Day 07: Project Risk Assessment Pack
Objective: Scan project launch sheets to catch dependencies and timelines before they trigger severe delays.

Step 1: Catching Gaps

Plaintext
"You are an expert project manager. Review the attached project details for our upcoming [product/service launch]. Identify any hidden dependencies, resource gaps, or process steps completely missing from this plan."
Step 2: Risk Scoring

Plaintext
"Focus heavily on the [insert specific gap found in Step 1]. Break down how this single gap could cascade into a critical timeline blocker, and assign a risk level (Low, Medium, High)."
📊 Day 08: Deep Research & Infographic Pipeline
Objective: Build an end-to-end multi-prompt system that takes a raw trend and turns it into a structured, cited report with visual layouts.

Step 1: Market Landscape Mapping

Plaintext
"I am analyzing the market position for [insert product/service]. What are the top 3 emerging industry trends or consumer pain points shaking up this space right now?"
Step 2: Verified Live Search Extraction (Best ran in Perplexity or Gemini Deep Research)

Plaintext
"Run a deep research dive into [specific trend from Step 1]. Use reputable web sources from the past 12-24 months. Extract concrete data points, metrics, adoption rates, and provide hyperlinked source citations."
🧠 Day 09: NotebookLM Source Tutoring Pack
Objective: Build an isolated knowledge vault out of your own PDFs, links, or YouTube recordings to extract zero-hallucination insights.

Step 1: The One-Page Executive Core Summary

Plaintext
"Analyze all uploaded documents. Generate a strict one-page summary breaking down: 1. The primary thesis statement. 2. Top 5 critical data discoveries. 3. Immediate real-world applications. Append source citations to every bullet point."
Step 2: Critical Logic Auditor

Plaintext
"Act as an aggressive peer reviewer. Scan these uploaded files and identify any weak evidence, logic loops, unbacked industry assumptions, or conflicting arguments. Give me 3 hard questions to test the structural integrity of this text."
